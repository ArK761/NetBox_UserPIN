Four-eyes approval

Who switches it on: whoever can change Settings (the master, a CORE deputy with the permission).
When: when a sensitive change is to be confirmed by a second person.
Depends on: at least two active CORE deputies with a PIN and 2FA; without them it cannot be switched on.

Switching on and categories

Settings → Four-eyes approval (off by default). After switching on you choose what it applies to:

  • Switching on needs at least 2 active CORE deputies. The deputies are told (the e-mail Four-eyes approval switched on).
  • Switching off is not possible while a department exists. Switching off needs two people and ends the CORE delegation: the deputies are told by e-mail and from then on manage only their own PIN.

The course of a request

  1. The requester makes the change: a request Four-eyes request #N with a reason appears. The requester confirms it with PIN + 2FA (Confirm your request).
  2. Another eligible person gets a notice in the bell and an e-mail. The eligible are: the master and CORE deputies; for a department request also its head and delegates.
  3. They open Approvals (or the link) and Confirm or Reject with their own PIN + 2FA. A person cannot vote twice.
  4. The request page refreshes itself and both see the confirmations (who, time, IP). Right after the second confirmation the change is executed.
  • A request is valid for Approval request valid minutes (1440 = 24 h by default; 5 to 10080) and then expires.
  • The requester can cancel it. A rejection is announced by e-mail.
  • Break-glass (off by default, Allow break-glass for the master): in an emergency a superuser may execute a request alone with a reason (Execute alone). All administrators and delegates get an e-mail and it is marked in the audit log.

The page Approvals (four eyes): Waiting (change, requested by, confirmed, valid until; Confirm / Open) and History (change, requested by, confirmed by, status, finished). If approval is off, the page says so.

Audit log

Who sees it: CORE (master and deputies) everything; heads and delegates only the events of the people of their department and their own.
When: when investigating who did what and when.
Depends on: nothing: it is always written.

Menu User PIN → Audit log (PIN audit log). Records are only appended (they cannot be changed) and never contain a PIN, hash or code. The page shows 50 records per page and has the filters User (text: the account or whoever did the action) and Event (a choice of types). Columns: Time, User, Event, By, Scope, Detail.
