Global protection (ON / OFF) and keepers

Who: only protection keepers. While there is none, the first one is appointed by CORE / the master.
When: when everything that no group allows is to be denied; it is switched off in an emergency or for maintenance.
Depends on: at least one keeper with a PIN and 2FA. Without a keeper the protection cannot be switched on.

The Protection card is at the top of the Protected areas page.

ON and OFF

StateWhat applies
ONeverything that no group allows is denied to everybody except keepers and exceptions. PIN-only people see only their groups. It reaches every path: lists, details, REST API, GraphQL, search, widgets, menus and buttons. A superuser who is not a keeper sees none of Users, Groups, Permissions, Jobs, Background tasks… The own profile, bookmarks, notifications and preferences stay open, but only the user's own
OFFeverybody works by the NetBox permissions alone. PIN-only people can only change their PIN and are told that the function is switched off by the administrator

The buttons Switch the protection ON / Switch the protection OFF are seen and pressed only by a keeper. The change is written to the audit log and an e-mail goes to the other keepers.

Protection keepers

  • The card Protection keepers: the list, Remove and Appoint another keeper. While there is none, Appoint the first keeper (switches the protection on) stands there: appointing the first keeper switches the protection on.
  • A keeper must have a PIN and 2FA, the account must be active and must not be a PIN-only person. After the appointment the e-mail You are a protection keeper arrives.
  • A keeper is exempt from the denial (NetBox permissions apply to them), cannot turn off 2FA and cannot leave alone: another keeper has to take over.
  • The last keeper cannot be removed. If the account of the last keeper is deleted, the protection goes OFF and cannot be switched on until a keeper exists.
  • A keeper can do everything: areas, object types, section managers, exceptions, the ON/OFF switch and all PIN-only accesses. The limits for exceptions and managers do not apply to them.

E-mails about the changes in the protected areas

The card E-mails about the changes in the protected areas on the Protected areas page (set only by a keeper). When a user in a group of a protected area (not a keeper or a PIN-only person) adds, changes or deletes an object whose type the area protects:

  • the event Object changed in a protected area is written to the audit log (user, area, type and name of the object),
  • the keepers and the manager of the section that covers the object type get an e-mail (except the author of the change): the changed fields from → to.

Emergency on the server

python manage.py userpin_keeper                 # shows the keepers
python manage.py userpin_keeper --add <name>    # appoints a keeper
python manage.py userpin_default_deny --on      # switches the protection on (needs a keeper)
python manage.py userpin_default_deny --off     # switches the protection off

Every change by a command is written to the audit log and all keepers and superusers learn of it by e-mail (nothing happens quietly).

Exceptions

Who: only a keeper sets, changes and removes. A regular user (not a keeper, not a PIN-only person) can have an exception.
When: when one user is to work by NetBox permissions without the denial (e.g. a network administrator whom the protection limits).
Depends on: the protection switched on (while it is off nothing is taken away).

A user with an exception is not bound by the protection: they work with the permissions NetBox gives them. Their groups, accesses and roles stay as they are and apply again when the exception is taken away or ends. A superuser with an exception sees everything without the PIN. The Exceptions card is on the Protected areas page.

An exception on and off (the switch)

At every user there is a slide switch. The user stays in the list with all the settings (what they may use, the reason, the end) and you switch the exception on and off with one click, without removing it:

SwitchWhat applies
Exception onthe user is ruled by NetBox and of the account pages and the API may use only what the keeper ticked (May use)
Exception offthe user is ruled by the PIN: the protection applies to them like to a regular user (groups, denials). The list of allowances is not used then, but it stays stored and applies again when switched on
  • Only a keeper can switch. The change takes effect at once (within 5 seconds at the latest), is written to the audit log and the other keepers get the e-mail Protection exception switched on / off.
  • Every row in the list has a tag Exception on (green), Exception off (yellow) or Ended (grey) and under the switch a text saying who rules them now (NetBox rules him, only what is allowed / The PIN rules him).

The end of an exception (date and time)

  • At every user there is the field Until with a date and time and a Save button: you can change the end at any time. An empty field = no end. A new end must be in the future.
  • When the end passes the PIN rules the user again at once (within 5 seconds) and the system job (every 5 minutes) moves the switch to Exception off and writes it to the audit log; the other keepers get the e-mail Protection exception ended. The row stays in the list with the tag Ended.
  • You switch an ended exception on again by first setting a new end (or clearing it) and then switching it on. Switching on with a past end is refused (The exception has ended: set a new end (or none) before switching it on).

Searching and a list for many users

  • Above the list there is Search (name, account or reason) and the filter State: All / Exception on / Exception off / Ended. The Clear button resets it. After changing a switch or an end the search is kept.
  • The list is paged by 25. At the top there is a summary (N exceptions: X on, Y off).
  • When adding there is a search field above the choice of the user: you type and the list narrows; when one is left it is chosen by itself.

Adding an exception

The form: User (keepers and those who already have an exception are not offered), Until (optional) (the end; must be in the future), Why (optional) (up to 200 characters) and the button May use with the items (see below), then Set the exception. A new exception is on. The other keepers get the e-mail Protection exception: name and it is written to the audit log. If the user is in groups the page warns that they do not apply while the exception lasts. Adding the same user again is refused (changes are made in the list).

The exception concerns the protection, not the account pages and the API. Those the user may use only as the keeper allowed:

  • Under the name of every user there is an arrow ▾ May use that unfolds the check boxes with a Save button, and small tags: green ✓ allowed, red ✗ not allowed.
  • What is not allowed disappears from the menu and the tabs and the direct address returns 403 (Not allowed for this account, with the names of the keepers).
  • NetBox has no link to the password in the user menu (only a tab of the profile). While the password is allowed, the plugin adds one to the menu.
  • A keeper can always do everything. While the protection is off nothing is taken away. A superuser who is not a keeper is not limited either while there is no keeper. With the exception off the list of allowances is not used (the PIN rules the user).
Why it matters. NetBox gives every signed-in user the right to their own API tokens (DEFAULT_PERMISSIONS), so a user without any rights sees Admin → API Tokens in the menu. A token carries only the permissions of its owner, but the plugin does not judge requests with a token: a user with rights could read beyond their areas through a token. The list of allowances solves it without touching the NetBox configuration.

Section managers

Who appoints and removes: a keeper (while there is none, CORE / the master). A section manager does not appoint or remove managers.
When: when somebody other than a keeper is to decide about a part of an area: groups, fields, members, requests.
Depends on: the manager must have a PIN and 2FA and be a regular NetBox account (not a superuser, not a keeper, not a PIN-only person).
  • A section = the object types of the area; nothing ticked = the whole area. At a manager there is the button Save the section; you appoint a new one with Appoint the section managers (the form Section + a choice of users).
  • A manager has full power over their section: groups, fields, members and requests. The keepers stand above them. A department role is not needed.
  • Requests for their types go to them and the keepers; every decision is e-mailed to the requester, the keepers and the manager.
  • PIN-only accesses made by a keeper belong to the keepers; a manager sees only those under them and creates them only into the groups of their section (the keepers get an e-mail). A keeper can move an access under a manager.
  • A manager of a whole area keeps their rights over the area (targets, profiles); creating an area, adding and removing targets and managers belong to the keepers. The last manager of an area can be removed only by a keeper (CORE / the master while there is no keeper cannot remove them, except a manager who has meanwhile become a superuser or a keeper).
  • Appointing a manager automatically allows them to use a PIN (unless the PIN administrator explicitly denied it: then the appointment fails).
  • When appointing, the page shows what the manager lacks (Has a PIN, Has 2FA, Is not a superuser or a protection keeper, Is a NetBox account).
  • Emergency on the server: python manage.py userpin_area_owners <area-key> --set name1 name2 (written to the audit log; it refuses people who could not be managers unless you give --force).
