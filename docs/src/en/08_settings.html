Settings

Who sees them: CORE (master and deputies). Nobody else. (When there are keepers and active CORE deputies, a superuser who is not a CORE deputy does not see them.)
Who can change: the master and a CORE deputy with the permission Can edit settings. Others see the page marked Read-only view.
Confirmation: saving needs the PIN (and 2FA with Require 2FA also for settings on). With four-eyes approval and the category "settings" it creates a request.
When: after the installation and when the company policy changes.

Menu User PIN → Settings (PIN settings). The page has the cards: General, Access & PIN policy, Rotation, Unlock, Protection, Four-eyes approval, Protected areas and at the bottom Encryption key (the active key, the fingerprint, the configured keys). Mail (SMTP, domains, notifications, recovery of a forgotten PIN) is on its own page Mail; Deny everything not allowed and the other protection options are on the page Protected areas; the rules of the PIN-only people are on the page People with a PIN only.
