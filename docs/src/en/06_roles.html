Roles: CORE and departments

Who: the master (superuser) appoints CORE deputies. CORE creates departments and appoints heads. Heads invite delegates. A delegate manages the members without a role.
When: when PINs are managed not by one person but by several; after the installation the master alone is enough.
Depends on: a PIN and 2FA of every role holder, an e-mail in a verified domain (the invitation), mail set up, a running netbox-rq (reminders, expiry of invitations).
CORE        master (superusers) + CORE deputies        manages everybody
Department  head + delegates (min. 2) + members          the PINs of the members of the department

An invitation to a role

Every role (CORE deputy, head, delegate, temporary head, replacement) is an invitation:

  1. The inviter chooses a user and the validity of the invitation: 12, 24, 36 or 48 hours (the page rejects any other value).
  2. The invitee gets an e-mail explaining the role and the responsibility, with a link and a code, and a notice in the bell.
  3. The invitee opens the invitation. If they have no PIN or 2FA yet, the page first sends them to set them up (First set up PIN and 2FA) and then they return.
  4. The invitee accepts the invitation with the code from the e-mail, their PIN and a 2FA code (the code may be entered at most 5 times), or declines it. Only by accepting does the role start.

Who can be invited: an active user who is not a superuser (the master needs no role), has no denied PIN, has an e-mail in a verified domain and mail is set up (otherwise the invitation would not arrive; the page refuses and says why), has no same role or open invitation. A CORE deputy cannot hold a department role; a person in another department must be moved first. A delegate can be invited only when the department has a head: first the head, then the delegates. If an invitation expires it can be sent again (Send again) or cancelled.

Minimum and the end of a role

  • Minimum: with four-eyes approval on at least 2 CORE deputies; every department a head and at least 2 delegates.
  • Resignation: the role holder gives the role up in My roles. Below the minimum the role stays in force (resigning) until a replacement accepts. The superiors are told by e-mail (the head for a delegate, CORE for a head, the master for a CORE deputy).
  • Removal / replacement (the button Remove / replace): below the minimum only together with a replacement, the old person stays until the replacement accepts. A regular head ends at once (CORE manages the department meanwhile, until a new head accepts the invitation).
  • A department without an active head reports The department has no active head – CORE manages it until a head accepts the invitation.

CORE deputies (the page CORE (deputies), the master only)

  • Invite a deputy (with the option Can edit settings).
  • At a deputy: Allow settings / Make read-only (switches whether they may change Settings and Mail), Remove / replace, Send again, Cancel. Below is History.
  • The page shows the number of active deputies and whether four-eyes approval is on (it needs at least 2).
  • A deputy sees all users and departments (after a PIN + 2FA confirmation), creates departments and appoints heads. They do not manage the master or other deputies.

Departments

The Departments page: a table (department, head, delegates, members; no head – managed by CORE), moves waiting for a decision, the number of active users without a department (only CORE manages them) and the form New department (the name must be unique). The department detail:

Moves between departments

  • CORE moves directly. A head asks for a move (Ask for the move); the head of the other department decides (CORE for people without a department; if the other department has no head, CORE decides).
  • A request is valid for Approval request valid minutes (24 hours by default); the decider approves or rejects it, the requester can cancel it. The decider gets an e-mail and a notice. Only one move can wait per person.
  • The moved person, both heads and both departments are told by e-mail. A delegate needed for the minimum cannot be moved before a replacement arrives.
  • A member can also be assigned from the form Get somebody into this department.

My roles

Everybody sees which department they belong to, their roles (what it means and the responsibility), a waiting invitation, the notice of a temporary hand-over and the state of a resignation, and may give a role up. The role is synchronised into NetBox permissions named User PIN: …: do not edit them by hand.
