People with a PIN only (PIN-only)

Who creates and manages them: a protection keeper (all) and a section manager (only those under them and into the groups of their section). The menu People with a PIN only is seen by a keeper and a manager.
When: for people who have no NetBox account and are to see or change only a part (e.g. a technician on tenants).
Depends on: mail set up (the personal link comes by e-mail; without it you see it on the screen), a group in a protected area, the protection switched on (while it is off the person can only change the PIN), a running netbox-rq (summaries of changes).

Behind a person stands a technical user PinUser0001, PinUser0002 … (a number is never reused or changed; the alias and description are free texts and nothing is renamed in NetBox). The technical account has no usable password: it can sign in only through the PIN page. Nobody sees it in user lists, drop-downs or the API. The NetBox change log shows the technical user.

The person's first sign-in

  1. The person gets a personal link by e-mail (valid 7 days; Send the link again issues a new one and the old one stops). The link opens the page Hello UserPIN : PN (the person is called by the personal number).
  2. They choose their own PIN: by the PIN rules (by default 8 to 64 characters, not only digits, at least 4 different characters; the administrator can also require a letter and a digit, see below). For a person with a personal number the PIN need not be unique. Nobody can read it, not even the administrator.
  3. The button Set the PIN and sign in. If the invitation expired: Ask the administrator for a new one.

If mail is not set up, the link is shown to the administrator on the screen (once, Give this personal link yourself).

The personal number and the sign-in rules

  • The personal number (PN) is entered by the administrator when the person is created (required) or later in the person's row (block Alias and description, field Personal number (sign-in)). It is unique and cannot be changed once saved; the person gets an e-mail about it.
  • The person is called UserPIN : PN everywhere: in the list of people (the second line ID: 1 is the number of the technical account), in the audit log (user and done by), in e-mails, in the top menu, on the person's welcome page and on the blocking pages. The name is not shown in the list.
  • The card Sign-in and PIN rules (for everybody) (page People with a PIN only, keepers only): The personal number is a must, Shortest PIN (6 to 64), The PIN must contain a letter, The PIN must contain a digit.
  • The personal number as a must cannot be switched on while an access has no number (the page lists who lacks it). Once on, everybody gets the e-mail Your NetBox sign-in is changing and the PIN alone no longer works; the sign-in page has two fields (Personal number and PIN) and a big red notice.
  • A stricter PIN rule (a longer PIN, a letter or a digit required): everybody gets an e-mail and must choose a new PIN at the next sign-in with the old data; the change page shows the rule and an example (Kava2026Moja).
  • While the number is not a must, a person without a number signs in with the PIN alone (such PINs stay unique, Choose another PIN). A person with a number can sign in with the number and the PIN; a wrong number or PIN gives the same message The personal number or the PIN is not correct.

What the person sees: only their areas

A person with a PIN only always lives in mode B (whatever the settings say): they see only the areas where they are in a group. If in none, they see nothing. Items outside their groups (e.g. Devices) disappear from the menu and have no “door” either.

Regular sign-in: one shared address

  • Everybody uses one shared page (/pin/ by default) and enters the personal number and the PIN (while the number is not a must, people without a number enter only the PIN: the PIN tells who they are). The address is chosen by a keeper on the page (Address for signing in with a PIN: part A and optionally B, e.g. /tenants/ or /aqer/x2/; case-insensitive). The person saves it as a bookmark; it does not change until a keeper changes it (after a change they must save the new one).
  • The personal link from the e-mail works too (the person is known there).
  • If they forgot the PIN: I forgot my PIN – send me a link to set a new one (the address; the link is valid 1 hour, once, at most one per minute; it does not work for a disabled or ended access).

What the person sees and cannot do

  • The welcome page: You are signed in as UserPIN : PN, the PIN validity (days and hours), the working time (Your working time), after the end It is over: one more save, then you only read, the inactivity after which the session ends, and the list of accesses (group X, you may…, valid until …) with the button Continue. Without a group: No access has been given to you yet. You can only change your PIN. With the protection off: The function is switched off by the administrator for now. You can only change your PIN.
  • The user menu: My access, Change my PIN, Sign out. They have no password, 2FA, API or token; they are never a keeper, a manager or an exception. The account pages and the pages of the plugin are closed to them. They cannot make access requests.
  • The drop-downs of forms work for them (brief API lists); the rest of the API is closed. They save their own table columns.
  • Changing the PIN (Change my PIN): the current PIN, the new PIN, the new PIN again. After the expiry or a forced change it is asked before continuing.

Wrong PINs

The rules are Wrong PINs per step (5 by default; 1 to 50) and Waits after each step (seconds) (10,20,30 by default; 1 to 10 waits of 1 to 3600 s). They are set for everybody (Rules for wrong PINs (defaults)) and may also be own for one person; empty = the defaults.

The person's session

The session ends after Idle minutes (30 by default; 5 to 240) or after at most Session hours (8 by default; 1 to 24), at the end of the access validity, after disabling, or when a request arrives from an address outside the allowed IP list. It is set for every access separately. After signing out the person returns to the shared PIN page.

The administrators get the changes either as a summary every N minutes (Summary every: 5, 15, 30, 60, 120, 240 minutes or once a day; 60 by default), or at every change (one e-mail per change): never both at once. The subject at every change: UserPIN : PN made a change; the body: time, object and the changed fields (from → to). The e-mail is sent after the save through the netbox-rq queue, so saving stays fast; the summaries are processed by the system job every 5 minutes. A keeper sets it in the card E-mails (for everybody).

When a section manager works with an access, the keepers are told by e-mail (nothing happens quietly). An access made by a keeper belongs to the keepers until it is moved under a manager.

Working time

Who sets it: a keeper (the general rules and the rules of the individual accesses).
When: when PIN-only people are to work only in working hours.
Depends on: Limit the working time of the PIN-only accesses switched on (off by default), the holidays library for the holidays, the time zone of the server (TIME_ZONE in NetBox).

The rules

The general rules are on the People with a PIN only page (the card Working time (for everybody)): Limit the working time, Country (holidays) (SK by default), Days (Mon–Fri by default), From (06:00) and To (16:00). At every access you can choose:

OptionMeaning
the general rulesthe rule of the page applies (if the limit is on; otherwise no limit)
own rulesan own country, days and hours (e.g. a Hungarian technician with the Hungarian holidays)
no limitthe person has no limit at all
  • At every person it is written how the time is set: Working time: set individually (…), Working time: off (no limit) or Working time: the general time applies (…) (if the general time is switched off, it says that it is switched off and that there is no limit).
  • Changed days or hours with the general rules chosen are not saved and the plugin says so: The general working time applies to this access, so the changed days or hours cannot be saved. Choose "own rules" … or "no limit". The own time is saved only with own rules chosen.
  • Going back to the general rules (from own or from off) drops the person's own values: the person takes the time of the general rules (also when the general time is changed later).

Countries: SK, CZ, HU, AT, PL, DE, UA, HR, SI, RO, BG, RS, GB, IE, FR, ES, IT, NL, BE, CH, SE, NO, DK, FI, PT, GR, LT, LV, EE, US, CA. The public holidays come from the holidays library (works offline, needs pip install holidays; without it the page reports The library of public holidays is not installed and only days and hours are checked). The holidays follow the law the library implements: for example in Slovakia in 2026 the days off are 1 Jan, 6 Jan, Good Friday, Easter Monday, 1 May, 5 Jul, 29 Aug, 1 Nov and 24, 25, 26 Dec, while 8 May, 1 Sep, 15 Sep and 17 Nov are working days. The plugin has no vacations or custom company days.

What happens outside the time

  • The person can sign in and read, but cannot save or change anything: no saving, adding, editing, deleting or writing through the API. Forbidden requests are redirected with a message (Outside your working time (…) you can only read), the API answers 403.
  • The working time is always visible: on the welcome page and in the top bar, in the person's language. On a holiday Today is a public holiday (name) is written before it.
  • When the time ends while they work: 30 minutes and one save, then only reading.
  • The state of a day: in time / just ended (30 minutes) / before the start / after the end / a day off or a holiday.
